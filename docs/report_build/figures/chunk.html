Chunking

Section, size, overlap, then a same-section merge

This is structural packing. It is not a recursive character splitter and it is not an embedding-based splitter. LLM chunking exists and is switched off.

Group under the heading

  1. Start in the section named Introduction.
  2. A heading closes the open group and becomes the next section name.
  3. Keep adding paragraph blocks until the next one would pass 1,200 characters.

Then cut, check, and merge

  1. Carry the last 100 characters, from a word boundary, into the next group.
  2. If one group is still too long, cut on a blank line, a newline, or a sentence end.
  3. Reject an empty chunk, a chunk under 20 characters, or a near-duplicate at similarity 0.92.
  4. Merge a chunk under 300 characters into the next chunk of the same section, up to 1,560 characters.
